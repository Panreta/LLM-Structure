---
title: Multiply by likelihood
kind: layer
order: 4
tex: '\times\, p(x \mid \theta)'
---

The likelihood says how well each $$\theta$$ explains the observed $$x$$. Viewed as a function of $$\theta$$ it is *not* a probability distribution over $$\theta$$: it need not sum to 1.

Example: $$p(H \mid \text{fair}) = 0.5$$ and $$p(H \mid \text{biased}) = 0.9$$.
