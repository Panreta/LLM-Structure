---
title: Likelihood
order: 2
tex: 'p(x \mid \theta) = \theta^{h}(1-\theta)^{t}'
---
The brown dotted curve: how probable the observed flips are for each $$\theta$$. Here $$x$$ is a sequence with $$h$$ heads and $$t$$ tails.

As a function of $$\theta$$ the likelihood is not a probability density. The figure rescales it to area 1 so it can share the axes with the others.

Example: 7 heads and 3 tails give $$\theta^7(1-\theta)^3$$, which peaks at the observed frequency $$0.7$$.
