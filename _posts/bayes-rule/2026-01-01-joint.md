---
title: Joint
order: 3
tex: 'p(x, \theta) = p(x \mid \theta)\,p(\theta)'
---
Prior times likelihood, point by point. In the example it is proportional to $$\theta^8(1-\theta)^4$$.

It has exactly the shape of the posterior, but its total area is $$p(x)$$, a tiny number. So it isn't drawn at its true scale.
