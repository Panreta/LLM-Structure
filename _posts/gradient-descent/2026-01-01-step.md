The blue path. For this $$f$$ the update acts on each coordinate separately:

$$x_1 \leftarrow (1-\eta)\,x_1, \qquad x_2 \leftarrow (1-6\eta)\,x_2$$

When $$\eta > \tfrac16$$, the factor $$1 - 6\eta$$ is negative, so $$x_2$$ flips sign every step: the zigzag across the valley.
