---
title: Step
kind: layer
order: 3
tex: '\mathbf{x}_{k+1} = \mathbf{x}_k - \eta\,\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_k)'
---

Move a distance proportional to the learning rate $$\eta > 0$$ against the gradient.

Example: $$f(x) = x^2$$ has $$\nabla f = 2x$$, so

$$x_{k+1} = (1 - 2\eta)\,x_k$$

This converges to 0 exactly when $$0 < \eta < 1$$. It oscillates for $$\tfrac12 < \eta < 1$$ and diverges for $$\eta > 1$$: the learning rate matters.
