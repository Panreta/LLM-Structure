tex: 'f(\mathbf{x}) = \tfrac12\left(x_1^2 + 6x_2^2\right)'
---
The contour lines are the level sets $$f = c$$ of an elongated bowl. The curvature is 1 along $$x_1$$ and 6 along $$x_2$$: the Hessian is $$\mathrm{diag}(1, 6)$$, so $$\mu = 1$$ and $$L = 6$$.

That mismatch, the condition number $$\kappa = L/\mu = 6$$, is what makes the valley hard to descend.
