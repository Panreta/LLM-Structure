---
title: Repeat
kind: core
order: 2
tex: '\times\, T \text{ steps}'
---

Apply the step again and again, until $$\lVert \nabla f(\mathbf{x}_k) \rVert < \varepsilon$$ or a step budget runs out.

Guarantee for a convex $$f$$ whose gradient is $$L$$-Lipschitz, with $$\eta \le 1/L$$:

$$f(\mathbf{x}_T) - f^\star \le \frac{\lVert \mathbf{x}_0 - \mathbf{x}^\star \rVert^2}{2\eta T}$$
