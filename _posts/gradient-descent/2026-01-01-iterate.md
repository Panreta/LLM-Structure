The blue dots. They converge exactly when both factors have magnitude below 1, $$\lvert 1-\eta \rvert < 1$$ and $$\lvert 1-6\eta \rvert < 1$$, that is when

$$0 < \eta < \frac{2}{L} = \frac13$$

Push the learning rate past $$\tfrac13$$ and the iterates fly off.
