The step needs two things: the gradient *and* the current point itself. The point $$\mathbf{x}_k$$ skips past the gradient computation and goes straight into the update, like a residual connection: $$\mathbf{x}_{k+1} = \mathbf{x}_k + (\text{correction})$$.
