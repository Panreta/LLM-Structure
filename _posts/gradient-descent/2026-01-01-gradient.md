---
The brown arrow, drawn at unit length. It is perpendicular to the contour through $$\mathbf{x}_0$$ and points uphill.

By the first-order Taylor expansion,

$$f(\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{h}) \approx f(\mathbf{x}) + \nabla f(\mathbf{x})^\top \mathbf{h}$$

so stepping in the direction $$-\nabla f$$ decreases $$f$$ fastest.
