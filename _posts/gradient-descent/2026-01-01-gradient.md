The gray arrows are $$-\nabla f$$ across the plane, and the brown arrow is $$\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0)$$ at unit length. The gradient is perpendicular to the contours and points uphill.

By the first-order Taylor expansion,

$$f(\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{h}) \approx f(\mathbf{x}) + \nabla f(\mathbf{x})^\top \mathbf{h}$$

so among small steps of a given length, $$-\nabla f$$ decreases $$f$$ fastest.

Notice where the arrows point: almost straight across the valley, toward the nearest wall's floor, and hardly at all along the valley toward $$\mathbf{x}^\star$$. The steep direction dominates the gradient. That is the problem the rest of the story works on.
