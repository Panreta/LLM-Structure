---
title: Gradient
kind: tensor
order: 4
tex: '\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_k) \in \mathbb{R}^n'
---

The vector of partial derivatives $$\partial f / \partial x_i$$. It points in the direction of steepest increase.

By the first-order Taylor expansion,

$$f(\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{h}) \approx f(\mathbf{x}) + \nabla f(\mathbf{x})^\top \mathbf{h}$$

so $$\mathbf{h} = -\eta\,\nabla f(\mathbf{x})$$ decreases $$f$$ by about $$\eta \lVert \nabla f \rVert^2$$ when $$\eta$$ is small.
