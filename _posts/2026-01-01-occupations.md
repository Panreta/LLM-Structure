A skip connection: the occupation vector $$\mathbf{n}$$ bypasses the network and goes straight to the row-selection step.

Division of labor: the network decides the values of the orbitals, $$\Phi(\mathbf{s})$$; the occupations decide which rows of them survive into the determinant.
