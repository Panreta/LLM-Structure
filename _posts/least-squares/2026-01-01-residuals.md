---
title: Residuals
order: 3
tex: 'r_i = y_i - \hat y_i'
---
The brown vertical segments. They are vertical, not perpendicular to the line, because only $$y$$ is assumed to carry error.

Example: $$\mathbf{r} = (0,\ 0.2,\ -0.6,\ 0.6,\ -0.2)$$. With an intercept in the model, the residuals always sum to 0.
