The targets skip the Gram matrix and enter the solve as its right-hand side, $$X^\top \mathbf{y}$$.

Division of labor: the features alone decide the matrix of the system, and the targets decide what it is solved for.

Example: $$X^\top \mathbf{y} = (5, 6)$$.
