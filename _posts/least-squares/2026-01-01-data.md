---
title: Data
kind: plain
order: 5
tex: '(X, \mathbf{y}), \; X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}'
---

$$m$$ observations of $$n$$ features. A column of ones gives the fit an intercept.

Example: the points $$(0,1), (1,2), (2,2)$$ give

$$X = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{y} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$$
