---
title: Gram matrix
kind: tensor
order: 4
tex: 'X^\top X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}'
---

Inner products between the feature columns. It is symmetric and positive semidefinite, and it is invertible exactly when the columns of $$X$$ are linearly independent.

Example:

$$X^\top X = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 3 \\ 3 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$$
