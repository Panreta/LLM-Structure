The fitted values. Geometrically, $$\hat{\mathbf{y}}$$ is the orthogonal projection of $$\mathbf{y}$$ onto the column space of $$X$$, so the residual is perpendicular to every column:

$$X^\top(\mathbf{y} - \hat{\mathbf{y}}) = \mathbf{0}$$

Example: $$\hat{\mathbf{y}} = (\tfrac76, \tfrac53, \tfrac{13}{6})$$, with residual $$(-\tfrac16, \tfrac13, -\tfrac16)$$.
