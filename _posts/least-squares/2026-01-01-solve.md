---
title: Normal equations
order: 5
tex: 'X^\top X\,\beta = X^\top \mathbf{y}'
---
Setting the gradient of the squared error to zero,

$$\nabla_\beta \lVert \mathbf{y} - X\beta \rVert^2 = -2X^\top(\mathbf{y} - X\beta) = \mathbf{0}$$

gives a small linear system. Example:

$$\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 10 \\ 10 & 30 \end{pmatrix}\beta = \begin{pmatrix} 13 \\ 34 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \hat\beta = (1,\ 0.8)$$

In practice, solve it with a Cholesky or QR factorization rather than an inverse.
