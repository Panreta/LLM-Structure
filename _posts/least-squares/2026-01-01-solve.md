The normal equations. They come from setting the gradient of the squared error to zero:

$$\nabla_{\beta} \lVert \mathbf{y} - X\beta \rVert^2 = -2X^\top(\mathbf{y} - X\beta) = \mathbf{0}$$

In practice, solve with a Cholesky or QR factorization rather than forming the inverse.
