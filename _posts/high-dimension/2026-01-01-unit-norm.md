---
title: Unit-norm lemma
order: 1
tex: 'P\bigl(\lvert \lVert u \rVert^2 - 1 \rvert \ge \varepsilon\bigr) \le 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{n\varepsilon^2}{8}\right)'
---
Take $$u = (u_1, \dots, u_n)$$ with independent entries $$u_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \tfrac1n)$$, so $$\mathbb{E}\lVert u \rVert^2 = 1$$. The lemma says $$\lVert u \rVert^2$$ is almost never far from 1. A random Gaussian vector lies close to the unit sphere, and the rest of the story builds on that.

**Chernoff's method.** Markov's inequality gives $$P(X \ge a) \le \mathbb{E}X / a$$ for $$X \ge 0$$. Apply it to $$e^{\lambda X}$$ and optimize over $$\lambda > 0$$:

$$P(X \ge a) \le \min_{\lambda > 0}\, e^{-\lambda a}\, \mathbb{E}\, e^{\lambda X}$$

**Upper tail.** Since $$n\lVert u \rVert^2 \sim \chi^2(n)$$, its moment generating function gives $$\mathbb{E}\,e^{\lambda \lVert u \rVert^2} = \left(\frac{n}{n - 2\lambda}\right)^{n/2}$$. The best choice $$\lambda = \frac{n\varepsilon}{2(1+\varepsilon)}$$ yields

$$\begin{aligned} P(\lVert u \rVert^2 \ge 1 + \varepsilon) &\le \exp\!\left(\tfrac{n}{2}\bigl(\log(1+\varepsilon) - \varepsilon\bigr)\right) \\ &\le \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{n\varepsilon^2}{8}\right) \end{aligned}$$

using $$\log(1+\varepsilon) - \varepsilon \le -\varepsilon^2/4$$ on $$(0, 1)$$.

**Lower tail.** The same argument with $$e^{-\lambda X}$$ gives $$\exp\!\left(\frac n2(\log(1-\varepsilon) + \varepsilon)\right)$$, which is even smaller. Adding the two tails gives the factor 2.

The curve is the exact density of $$\lVert u \rVert^2$$ and the brown tails are the probability being bounded. The readout compares the exact tail with the bound: loose, but it decays at the right exponential rate in $$n$$.
