In the eigenbasis, $$A$$ acts on each coordinate separately: $$c_i \mapsto \lambda_i c_i$$. Everything that is hard about a matrix becomes easy here. For example,

$$A^k = P\,D^k\,P^{-1}, \qquad D^k = \mathrm{diag}(\lambda_1^k, \dots, \lambda_n^k)$$

Example: the eigenvalues are $$\lambda_1 = 3$$ (eigenvector $$(1,1)$$) and $$\lambda_2 = 1$$ (eigenvector $$(1,-1)$$), so $$(2,1) \mapsto (6,1)$$.
