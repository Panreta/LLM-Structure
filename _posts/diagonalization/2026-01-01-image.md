---
title: Image
kind: tensor
order: 1
tex: 'A\mathbf{x} = P\,D\,P^{-1}\mathbf{x}'
---

The result of applying the matrix $$A$$ to $$\mathbf{x}$$, computed in three simple steps: change coordinates, scale, change back.

Running example:

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad A\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}$$
