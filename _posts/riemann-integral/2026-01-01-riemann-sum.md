---
title: Riemann sum
order: 3
tex: 'S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} f(x_i^*)\,\Delta x'
---
The rectangles. Rectangle $$i$$ has width $$\Delta x$$ and height $$f(x_i^*)$$, where the sample point $$x_i^*$$ (the dot) lies in $$[x_{i-1}, x_i]$$. Switch between left, midpoint and right sample points below the figure.

With right endpoints, $$x_i^* = i/n$$ and

$$S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{i^2}{n^2}\cdot\frac1n = \frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6n^2}$$

For $$n = 6$$ that is $$\tfrac{91}{216} \approx 0.4213$$.
