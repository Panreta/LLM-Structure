---
title: Riemann sum
kind: layer
order: 3
tex: 'S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} f(x_i^*)\,\Delta x'
---

Approximate the area by $$n$$ rectangles. Rectangle $$i$$ has width $$\Delta x$$ and height $$f(x_i^*)$$, where $$x_i^*$$ is any sample point in $$[x_{i-1}, x_i]$$.

Common choices are the left endpoint, the right endpoint or the midpoint. They give different $$S_n$$ but the same limit.
