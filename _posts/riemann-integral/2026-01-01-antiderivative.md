title: Antiderivative F

The shortcut, given by the fundamental theorem of calculus: if $$F^\prime = f$$ on $$[a,b]$$, then

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

No partition and no limit needed. Example: $$F(x) = x^2/2$$ gives $$F(1) - F(0) = \tfrac12$$, the same answer as the limit of Riemann sums.
