Raise $$n$$ with the slider: the rectangles get thinner, and the part of them sticking out above the curve shrinks to nothing.

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6n^2} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac13$$

Because $$x^2$$ is increasing, left sums sit below the area and right sums above it. The gap between them is $$\bigl(f(b) - f(a)\bigr)\Delta x = \tfrac1n$$.
