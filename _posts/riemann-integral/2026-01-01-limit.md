---
title: Limit
kind: core
order: 2
tex: '\lim_{n\to\infty}'
---

Refine the partition: let the number of pieces $$n \to \infty$$, so the width $$\Delta x \to 0$$ and the rectangles hug the curve.

Example: for $$f(x) = x$$ on $$[0,1]$$ with right endpoints,

$$S_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{i}{n}\cdot\frac{1}{n} = \frac{n+1}{2n} \longrightarrow \frac12$$
