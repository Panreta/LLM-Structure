---
title: Definite integral
order: 5
tex: '\int_a^b f(x)\,dx'
---
The shaded region: the signed area between the curve and the $$x$$-axis from $$a$$ to $$b$$. Area below the axis counts as negative.

It is defined as the limit of Riemann sums. For continuous $$f$$ that limit exists, and it doesn't depend on the choice of sample points. Here

$$\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac13$$
