---
title: Definite integral
kind: tensor
order: 1
tex: '\int_a^b f(x)\,dx'
---

The signed area between the graph of $$f$$ and the $$x$$-axis, from $$a$$ to $$b$$. Area below the axis counts as negative.

It is defined as the limit of the Riemann sums below. For continuous $$f$$ the limit exists, and it doesn't depend on where in each piece the sample points are taken.

Running example: $$\int_0^1 x\,dx = \tfrac12$$.
