---
title: Orbitals
kind: tensor
order: 3
tex: '\Phi(\mathbf{s}) \in \mathbb{R}^{2N\times N_e}'
---

The transformer's output, read as a matrix: one row per spin-orbital, one column per electron.

The final token vectors are mapped to the entries of $$\Phi(\mathbf{s})$$ by a learned output projection (check the paper for the exact map). Column $$k$$ is orbital $$k$$ evaluated on all $$2N$$ spin-orbitals.

Unlike a Slater determinant with fixed orbitals, these orbitals change with the configuration $$\mathbf{s}$$, since every token has seen every other token. That dependence is what lets the determinant above capture correlation.
