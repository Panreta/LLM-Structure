One basis configuration of the system.

$$\mathbf{n} \in \{0,1\}^{2N}$$: occupation of each of the $$2N$$ spin-orbitals ($$N$$ sites $$\times$$ 2 spin directions), with $$\sum_i n_i = N_e$$ electrons.

$$S_1^z, S_2^z$$: two additional spin variables that are part of the configuration. Replace this line with what they represent in the paper.
