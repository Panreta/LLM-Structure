---
title: Select occupied rows
kind: tensor
order: 2
tex: '\mathbf{n}\star\Phi(\mathbf{s}) \in \mathbb{R}^{N_e\times N_e}'
---

$$\mathbf{n}\star\Phi$$ keeps only the rows $$i$$ of $$\Phi$$ with $$n_i = 1$$ and drops the rest.

Exactly $$N_e$$ entries of $$\mathbf{n}$$ are 1, so $$N_e$$ rows survive, and each row has $$N_e$$ columns: the result is a square $$N_e\times N_e$$ matrix, which is what a determinant needs.

Example: $$2N = 4$$ spin-orbitals and $$\mathbf{n} = (1,0,1,0)$$, so $$N_e = 2$$. Keep rows 1 and 3 of the $$4\times 2$$ matrix $$\Phi$$ to get a $$2\times 2$$ matrix.
